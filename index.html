About Me



I am an Interior Design student at Rochester Institute of Technology. I am based in Philadelphia, PA and grew up in an artistic family. In my eyes, good design means getting to know an individual and expressing the things they love through abstract art and themes in everyday life. I am very interested in practicing more economical and sustainable design. Residential design is something that I have particular interest in.
